Pick a real photo

Choose a smiling selfie, a quiet morning, a walk in nature, a journal moment, family, or a church activity. Real human faces stop the scroll.

02

Follow the 5-part caption outline

Write naturally, as if you are talking with a close friend. Make the story personal and clear.

03

Add tags and hashtags

Use #AllThingsNew, #HopeStartsHere, and one hashtag related to your story. Tag your local church or family only.

5-part caption outline

  1. Hook: Name a relatable struggle or feeling.
  2. The turning point: Bring the burden to Jesus.
  3. The outcome: Share the fresh start or real peace He gave.
  4. The hope: Encourage someone and invite them to share with a close friend or family member.
  5. Hashtags: Add #AllThingsNew #HopeStartsHere and a story-related hashtag.

All Things NewJust now · Public

A while ago, I was feeling completely [overwhelmed by work / stressed about the future / exhausted by daily life / trapped in overthinking]. I kept trying to fix everything on my own, but honestly, it was wearing me out. That’s when I brought my mess to Jesus and asked Him for a real fresh start. He didn't change my circumstances overnight, but He gave me [a deep peace / clear direction / the strength to keep going / quiet rest for my mind] right in the middle of it. If you’re going through a heavy or tiring season right now, please know that fresh starts are real and hope is available. You don't have to figure it all out alone 🤍 Know someone who might need a little hope today? Feel free to share or send this post to a close friend or family member who's going through a tough week!
